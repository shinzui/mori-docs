## The scenario

A maintainer wants to leave repository automation running unattended. They
need to know what may reach a repository, why a reaction fired, what it did,
and whether the record will still be trustworthy after the operational logs
are gone.

## How it works

1. [`mori.dhall`](/docs/schema-guide) declares project identity; the separate `mori.automation.dhall` declares reactive behavior. Registration alone grants no execution.
2. Target-owned signal consent and emitter bounds limit cross-repository delivery.
3. [`mori automate explain`](/docs/commands/automate), reaction history, and [workflow traces](/docs/commands/workflow) expose matching and execution decisions.
4. Recorded config, provenance, and event history support projection rebuild and replay audit. See the [event store lifecycle guide](/docs/guides/event-store-lifecycle).

The source records **ExecutionPolicy enforcement** as discovered: its
`allowLocal`, `allowCI`, and `maxConcurrency` fields are declared but execution
does not consult them. `RunCommand` executes on the daemon host and is not a
sandbox. Selective physical event compaction is separately **blocked** pending
fact-preserving upstream support and proof.
