title: Registry Templates
description: Browse seihou templates across registered projects

---

`mori registry templates` provides a cross-project view of seihou templates
known to the registry. `mori registry list --has-templates` filters projects;
`mori registry templates` lists or shows individual templates.

The data comes from the `mori.seihou_templates` projection populated during
registration and registry projection.

## List Templates

```bash
mori registry templates list
mori registry templates list --tag haskell
mori registry templates list --namespace shinzui
mori registry templates list --json
```

Filters compose with AND semantics.

## Show A Template

```bash
mori registry templates show shinzui/haskell-kit/nix-flake
mori registry templates show shinzui/haskell-kit/nix-flake --json
```

The template reference has three segments:

```text
<namespace>/<project>/<template>
```

The detail view includes the template version, description, module path,
dependencies, required variables, and tags.

Templates can be declared explicitly in `mori.dhall`, or discovered during
`mori register --local` from `seihou-registry.dhall` or a root `module.dhall`
unless `--no-seihou-discovery` is passed.
